## Description

As part of our data analytics upskilling, this task focuses on selecting five unique products and understanding their performance through four types of data analytics: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.

Task Requirements:

For each of the five selected products, answer the following questions:

1. Descriptive Analytics – What happened? Identify the product's past performance, sales trends, revenue, and demand.
2. Diagnostic Analytics – Why did it happen? Identify possible reasons behind changes in sales, demand, or product performance.
3. Predictive Analytics – What might happen? Estimate future sales, demand, or product performance based on available information and trends.
4. Prescriptive Analytics – What should we do? Recommend actions to improve sales, manage inventory, and enhance product performance.
Task Expectations:

- Select five unique products.
- Answer all four analytical questions for each product.
- Provide relevant observations, explanations, predictions, and recommendations.
- Present the findings clearly and systematically.
Expected Outcome:

A structured analysis of five unique products demonstrating an understanding of all four types of data analytics and their practical applications in business decision-making.

## Description

Develop an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using sales data stored in Snowflake and connected to Power BI. Create the required database, schema, warehouse, and sales table in Snowflake, and insert 40 sample sales records covering four cities and four product categories.

Perform data calculations using DAX by creating calculated columns and measures for sales amount, total cost, gross profit, profit margin, total customers, total orders, and average order value.

Implement sales target tracking with a predefined target of ₹1,00,000, including target achievement percentage, target shortfall, target status, and a visual progress indicator.

Develop interactive visualizations to analyze revenue by city, product category, monthly sales trends, and category-wise revenue contribution. Add City and Date Range slicers to enable dynamic filtering across the dashboard.

The final dashboard should provide a clear overview of business performance, sales trends, profitability, and target achievement.

Expected Deliverables:

- Snowflake database, schema, warehouse, and sales table.
- 40 sample sales records.
- DAX calculated columns and measures.
- Sales KPI cards and target performance indicators.
- Four sales analysis charts.
- Interactive City and Date slicers.
- Completed Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard.

## Description

Analyze the developed Sales Performance Dashboard to identify meaningful business insights from the available sales data. The objective is not just to understand the numbers and visualizations, but also to explain what the data represents, why the findings are important to the business, and what actions should be taken to improve business performance.

Identify 5 important business insights using the dashboard KPIs, charts, and slicers. For each insight, provide an observation, business interpretation, and actionable recommendation.

Task Steps

1. Review the Sales Performance Dashboard, including KPI cards, sales by city, sales by product category, monthly sales trends, and profit analysis.
2. Identify 5 meaningful observations from the dashboard.
3. Explain the business importance of each observation.
4. Provide practical recommendations based on the findings.
5. Document all 5 insights in a structured format.
